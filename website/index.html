centrix is the platform reltix runs on, and it connects tenants directly with vetted local service partners, starting with plumbing. A tenant reports a problem once, and centrix hands the right partner the job with everything they need.

How a repair runs through centrix

Following one example: a leak under the kitchen sink in unit 4B.

  1. 1

    The tenant reports it

    In the tenant portal, the tenant describes the problem and adds a photo. centrix already knows the address, floor and contact details.

    “There's water under the kitchen sink.”

  2. 2

    centrix picks the partner

    centrix sees it's a plumbing job and chooses a vetted plumber who works in the area and has time.

    A plumber nearby with a slot tomorrow morning

  3. 3

    They agree a time

    The tenant agrees to share what the plumber needs and picks a slot. The plumber gets the job with photos and access details.

    Tuesday at 10:00, key with the caretaker

  4. 4

    The job is closed

    The plumber reports progress and uploads the invoice. centrix links both to the home, and the tenant confirms it's fixed.

    Repair and invoice saved on unit 4B
