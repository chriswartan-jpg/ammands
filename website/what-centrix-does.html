A leaking pipe used to take a dozen calls before anyone showed up. With centrix, the tenant reports it once and the right partner gets the job.

It already knows the home

centrix already knows every home reltix manages, who lives there and who owns it. So the moment a tenant reports a problem, it knows where it is and what kind of help is needed, and it connects the tenant straight to the right partner.

Nobody has to forward a message or read out an address over the phone.

The partner gets the whole picture

The partner gets the job with the address, the floor, photos of the problem and how to get in.

The tenant only answers what centrix doesn't know yet, agrees to share their details, and picks a time.
